Explanation about Age groups and Ranking points:

BFG WORLD SERIES ADULT 1VS1 B-BOYS OPEN AND ADULT 1VS1 B-GIRLS OPEN

  • Age group: 16 years old or older by December 31, 2026
  • Ranking points for WDSF World Ranking Adult

BFG WORLD SERIES YOUTH 1VS1 B-BOYS OPEN AND YOUTH 1VS1 B-GIRLS OPEN

  •  Age group: born between 2008 and 2013
  • Youth Olympics age group: Born November 14, 2008 - November 12, 2013
  • Ranking points for WDSF World Ranking Youth
  • Ranking points considered for the 2026 Youth Olympic Games in Dakar

BFG TEAM 3VS3 MIX OPEN

3 members with a minimum 1 Adult B-Girl born in 2010 or before, and recommended 1 Youth Athlete under 18 years old
