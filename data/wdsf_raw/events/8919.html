Visa Support
Participants who need visa support or an official invitation letter should contact the organiser at [email protected]. Please include your full name, nationality, passport details and confirmation of your WDSF registration.

Transportation
• The nearest airport is Palanga International Airport (PLQ), about 30 km from Klaipėda. 
• Vilnius (VNO) and Riga (RIX) airports are alternatives. 

Event Information
All event and participant information is on the official Global Wave Open website.
Times of each part will be published after registration closes.
An active WDSF e-card is required for all WDSF competitions.
Start numbers must be collected no later than 45 minutes before each part.

Registration and Entry Fees
• WDSF and DSE competitions: registration via WDSF
• Entry fee for WDSF Open Youth and Rising Stars: 30 € per athlete per competition
• Entry fee for DSE competitions (Juvenile, Junior I, Junior II): 20 € per athlete per competition
• WDSF and DSE competitors pay the entry fee online via the ticket system 
• National (LSŠF) competitions: registration via DanceSportInfo

Spectator Tickets
• Ticket: 15 €
• Seat at a table: +5 € per part, paid in cash at the venue
• Children under 5: free
• BUY tickets ONLINE
