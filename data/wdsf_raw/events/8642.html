Sunshine Coast - Maroochydore - Australia on 29/08/2026

Contact and information

  • https://www.jively.com.au
  • [email protected]

The Jively Events Fire Up & Golden Rose DanceSport Open 2026 will proudly host the WDSF Open Solo Latin Female events for Juvenile II, Junior II and Adult athletes for the first time in Australia. Held at Venue 114 on the beautiful Sunshine Coast, just north of Brisbane, the event combines DanceSport competition with the opportunity for athletes, families and supporters to enjoy one of Australia’s most relaxed and scenic coastal holiday destinations. The WDSF events will be presented alongside a full DanceSport Australia competition program offering all age groups and categories across 28-29 August 2026.

Invitations and Visa

International athletes, officials and supporters are responsible for checking and meeting Australia’s visa requirements before travelling. Visa information is available through the Australian Government here:
https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/entering-and-leaving-australia/entering-australia

To find the most appropriate visa, please use the Australian Government visa finder:
https://immi.homeaffairs.gov.au/visas/getting-a-visa/visa-finder

International athletes wishing to enter DanceSport Australia events must first complete the DSA International Athlete Registration form and obtain a DSA CID number: https://dancesport.org.au/entry/int/int1_start.php 

DSA International Athlete Blog for more information:  

https://dancesport.org.au/blog/2025-international-competitor-registration---update-1-july-2025.php

Where an invitation letter is required to support a visa application, athletes should complete the DSA international registration process first, then contact DanceSport Australia at [email protected] as early as possible.
